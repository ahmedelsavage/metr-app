Both parties are acknowledged to have the legal capacity to enter into contracts and perform legal acts. It has been agreed as follows:
Article One: The Second Party agrees to work for the First Party at the company’s premises or any other location affiliated with the company. The first three months shall constitute a probationary period, during which—or upon the conclusion of which—the First Party may terminate the Second Party’s services due to a lack of competence, without prior notice, compensation, or gratuity. The Second Party shall have no right to object to such a termination.
Article Two: This contract shall commence on the first day the Second Party begins work at the company.
